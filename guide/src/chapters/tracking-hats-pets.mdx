Three small things that make the long game friendlier: a sidebar that follows what you're chasing, a cheer when someone earns something big, and hats and pets to wear.

## Tracking

Open the Journal's **Objectives**, **Feats** or a **collection** page and press **Track** on the thing you're after. It goes on a sidebar on the right of your screen with its progress, like *Mine 32 coal 12/32*, and updates as you play. You can track dailies, the weekly, the server goal, any feat you can see, and the next milestone of a collection.

## The Wardrobe

The Journal's **Wardrobe** has four slots: a **title**, a **trail**, a **hat** and a **pet**. Wear one of each, mix them as you like, take any off again. Everything you own is listed there, whether a feat paid it, a reward table dropped it or you bought it.

## Hats

A hat rides on your head, so your helmet slot stays free and everyone nearby sees it. The shop only sells plain hats: a hat with an ability is power, and power is never for sale. Those come from feats and reward tables, and a few take real effort. An ability only works while the hat is on, and it stacks with your curios and relic perks.

## Pets

A pet walks (or floats) along behind you. You have one at a time; it can't be hurt, led or fed, and it quietly goes away when you log out, ride something or die, then comes back beside you. It gives you nothing but company. Some are bragging rights for deeds nobody forgets.
