A **feat** is a strange little milestone on something you already do: ten thousand moss, a hundred hoes, two hundred pufferfish. Each pays something that fits it, often a curio you can't get any other way, sometimes a title. They all count what you did before they existed, and some keep themselves secret until you earn them.

When you earn one, the whole server hears about it. The Journal's Feats page shows how far along you are on every one you can see.
