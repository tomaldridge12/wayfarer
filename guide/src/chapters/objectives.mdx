Not everything has to be a lifetime's work. **Objectives** are targets with a deadline, drawn from what fits where you are in the game: nobody gets "mine diamond ore" before they've smelted their first iron. Everything you already do counts towards them; nothing done before an objective was drawn does.

## Dailies and the weekly

<ObjectiveFacts part="rhythm" />

Each objective pays **tokens** and a roll from a reward table that fits it: a fishing daily pays fishing things, the weekly rolls from a better shelf. Some are collection pushes: a species new to your Almanac, a land you've never stood in, a kind of mob you've never fought.

Do every daily in a day and your **streak** grows. Miss a day and it **pauses** rather than resetting: life happens.

## The server goal

Each week the whole server shares one **server goal**, picked for the stage most of you have reached. Everything each of you does towards it adds up, and when it's met everyone who chipped in gets a goal reward and a **keepsake**: a medal in your hand that becomes your own head when you place it, naming the goal, the week and your share.

## Weekend events

<ObjectiveFacts part="events" />

Each event has its own goal for everyone, and its own chest of rewards for those who help.

## Tokens and the shop

Tokens are spent in the **Shop** page of your Journal, with a confirmation before anything is bought. They never buy power: relics, alloys, marks and curios are only ever earned.

<ObjectiveFacts part="shop" />

## Titles and trails

A **title** shows before your name in chat and the player list; a **trail** drifts behind you as you walk. Feats pay some, the shop sells others, and you pick one of each in your Journal's **Wardrobe**.
