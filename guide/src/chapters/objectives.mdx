Not everything has to be a lifetime's work. **Objectives** are targets with a deadline: do so many of something before the clock runs out.

## Dailies and the weekly

Every day you'll get a few **dailies**, and every week a bigger **weekly**. Finishing them pays **tokens**.

Do a daily every day and you build a **streak**. Miss a day and the streak **pauses** rather than resetting; life happens.

## Tokens and the shop

Tokens are spent in the shop on cosmetics and conveniences. They never buy power: nothing in the shop makes you hit harder or take less damage.

## The server goal

Each week the whole server shares one **server goal**: catch so many salmon between you, clear so many trial chambers. Everyone who chips in gets their own reward, including a **keepsake**, a placeable piece showing the goal and your share of it.

## Events

Some weekends bring an **event**: a theme with a twist to the rules and its own server goal. *The Drowned Tide*, say: double fishing loot all weekend, and five hundred salmon to land together.
