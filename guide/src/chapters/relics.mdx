Past the banner and the head, each family keeps something better: **relics**, weapons and armour made with that family in mind. Each one carries a **family edge** (extra damage to them, or less damage from them), a perk or two, and enchantments you can't get anywhere else.

They're not meant to replace your netherite. A relic is a sidegrade: brilliant against its own family, ordinary against everything else. Which is why you'll end up carrying more than one.

## How they work

- **Tiers.** Tier I relics come from climbing the ladder, Tier II from climbing much further. **Ascended** relics only drop from a family's mini boss. Each tier is its own item; nothing is ever upgraded in place.
- **The family edge** adds on top of a banner's boost. Stand in your zombie zone holding the Zombie Cleaver and the two stack.
- **Perks** never stack with themselves: two pieces with the same perk are no better than the strongest one.
- **Enchantments** marked in purple belong to relics alone; you won't find them on a table or from a villager.
