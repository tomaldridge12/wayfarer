A few vanilla annoyances Wayfarer simply sands down. None of them is a system; they're just kinder rules.

## Leather from rotten flesh

Zombies hand out rotten flesh by the stack and nothing in vanilla wants it, while leather, which every book needs, means running a cow farm. So the flesh is worth keeping: a handful of it crafts into a piece of leather. It's plain vanilla leather, and rotten flesh stays rotten flesh for everything else (wolves still eat it).

## What a death keeps

The tools in your hand are never the thing you lose to a creeper or a lava pool. What you carry in your hotbar, wear on your body and hold in your offhand comes back with you. The rest of your pack drops where you fell, and the server's grave collects it for you to fetch, as it always has. Items cursed with Curse of Vanishing still vanish, and your experience behaves exactly as in vanilla.

Your hotbar returns with every stack in the slot it was in, so your muscle memory still works.
