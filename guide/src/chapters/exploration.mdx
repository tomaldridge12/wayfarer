The world is enormous and mostly unvisited. Exploration is about to count for something.

## The Journal

Every wayfarer gets a **Journal**: a book of pages tracking what you've done. Ladders, objectives, a bestiary of the mobs and bosses you've met, an almanac of fish and crops, an atlas of biomes and structures, alloys made, treasures found. Fill a page far enough and it pays out titles and tokens.

This guide explains the rules. The Journal is your own record, and you'll find a link back here inside it.

## Treasures

Brush through archaeology sites and search structure chests, and you'll turn up **treasures**: gems, clay statues, fragments of something older. They're collectibles, and each one goes in the Journal.

## Finding your way

- A **wayfinder compass**, bought with experience at a cartography table, points to a biome or structure of your choice. Rarer places cost more.
- A **Guardian's Chart** points to the nearest structure whose guardian you haven't beaten yet.
- **Beacon kindling**, from the shop, lights a signal fire that calls a wandering trader to you.
