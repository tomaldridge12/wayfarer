The world is enormous and mostly unvisited. Here's how to find your way around it.

## The Wayfinder

Sneak and right-click a **cartography table** with a compass in your hand. Choose a structure, or any land you've already stood in somewhere, and pay in experience levels: the compass becomes a **Wayfinder** that points to the nearest one. While you hold it, the distance shows above your hotbar.

<ExplorationFacts />

If nothing is found in range, nothing is spent.

## The Guardian's Chart

From the same table: a chart that points to the nearest structure whose **guardian** you haven't beaten yet. Beat them all and it stays blank.

Free pointings from the Explorer's Compass curio or the shop are kept until you use them, so closing the screen never wastes one.

## Safe surface

Once the first Ender Dragon falls, hostile mobs stop spawning on the Overworld's open surface. Caves, the Nether and the End stay as dangerous as ever, and bosses still roam.

## Beacon kindling

Bought in the shop: drop it on a lit campfire and it burns as a tall signal, and before long a wandering trader comes to see what the fuss is about.
