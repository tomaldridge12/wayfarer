Some drops are rare enough to make a grown wayfarer weep. A wither skeleton skull is a two-and-a-half percent chance; plenty of people have farmed a fortress for an evening and gone home with nothing but bones.

Wayfarer puts a ceiling on that bad luck. For each of these items, the server counts your kills since your last one. If the count reaches the number below without the item dropping, the next kill **drops it for certain**. A real drop resets the count, so luck still helps, it just can't hurt you forever.

## The small print

- Only **counted** kills move the count: no spawners, no tamed mobs, the same rules as the ladders.
- The count is yours alone, kept by whoever lands the killing blow.
- **Looting** still raises your real chance on every kill, but the guarantee arrives at the same number either way.
- The number is simply one over the item's normal chance, rounded up: a 1% drop is guaranteed by the hundredth kill.
