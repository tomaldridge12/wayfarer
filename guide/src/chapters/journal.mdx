Every wayfarer carries a **Journal**. You get it the first time you join (lost it? `/wayfarer journal` opens it anywhere), and it fills itself in as you play: nothing to tick, nothing to write.

## What's in it

- **Ladders**: every count you're climbing, and the next thing at the top of it.
- **Objectives**, **Feats**, the **Shop** and your **Wardrobe**: see their own chapters.
- **Collections**: pages that ask you to find every one of something.

## Collections

A collection page shows what you've found with its picture, and what you haven't as a grey shape with a hint: where it lives, when it comes out, what keeps it. Fill a page far enough and it pays you, at milestones you can see on the page. Everything you did before a collection existed already counts, and anything you've ever held counts as found, so you don't need to keep it.

- **Bestiary**: every kind of creature you've felled, and every boss you've beaten. (Villagers, wandering traders and allays are never asked of you.)
- **Atlas**: every land you've stood in and every structure you've stepped inside, a tab for each dimension. Lands from the server's world generation, Terralith's included, are all here.
- **Cabinet**: vanilla's own collectibles: records, pottery sherds, smithing templates and banner patterns.
- **Curios**: every curio you've held.
- **Almanac** and **Treasures**: their own chapters.
