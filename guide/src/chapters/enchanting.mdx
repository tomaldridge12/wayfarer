The enchanting table offers three enchantments at a time, and they only change when you enchant something. If none of them suit you, the vanilla trick is to enchant a spare book at the cheapest slot just to see new offers, which wastes a book and a lapis every time. Wayfarer has a tidier way.

## The reshuffle

<EnchantingFacts />

The table opens straight away on the new offers, with a page-turn and a scatter of enchanting glyphs, and your bookshelves count as normal. At level 0 you're told you need a level. A plain right-click, or sneaking with an empty hand, opens the table the way it always did, so you never spend a level by accident.

The reshuffle is the one price in Wayfarer that's in **levels** rather than XP points. It mirrors the book trick it replaces, which also costs one level, and it never costs more than that at any level of yours.

## What the table never offers

A reshuffle only deals the table a new hand; it never edits what's in it. The enchantments Wayfarer gives as rewards stay rewards: they never turn up on the table, however often you reshuffle.
