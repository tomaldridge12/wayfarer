Some things out there have names. You'll know one when you see it: bigger than it should be, glowing, a health bar across the top of your screen and its name in colour. These are the **bosses**, and each is its own family on the ladder, with its own banner, its own trophy head and its own regalia.

## Stars

Every boss rolls **stars** when it appears, from one to five. Stars scale everything: its health, how hard it hits, how often it uses its tricks, and what it drops. The further from spawn you meet it, the more stars it's likely to have, with an occasional extra one for luck ({rules && `${Math.round(rules.extraStarChance * 100)}% of the time`}).

<StarTable />

Minecraft won't let any creature have more than 1,024 health, so the biggest bosses top out there however many stars they roll.

There are two ways to meet one.

- **Roaming bosses** rise from an ordinary mob's natural spawn in the right biome. They're capped at {rules?.roamingMaxStars} stars, and the same boss won't roam again in that world for {rules?.roamingCooldown} minutes.
- **Structure guardians** wait inside particular structures and appear when you walk in. They're always {rules?.guardianMinStars} stars or more, and each structure's guardian takes a while to come back. The wait starts when the guardian is *defeated*, not when it appeared, so one that stood waiting for hours isn't replaced the moment it falls.

Everyone who lands a hit gets their own loot roll, so bring friends. Bosses tied to a mob family are **mini bosses**: they drop that family's Ascended relics.
