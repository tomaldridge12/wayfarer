Feats, objectives and collections pay out in three kinds of **reward loot**: curios, special gear and supplies (plus the odd enchanted book).

## Curios

A **curio** is a small odd thing with one effect. **Charms** work from anywhere in your inventory; **tools** work on right-click and then rest for a while (the item shows the cooldown like an ender pearl does). Carrying two of the same curio does nothing more than one. Every curio you've held goes in the Journal's Curios page.

## Special gear

Named pieces with their enchantments already on them, most built on alloy gear (so they repair and upgrade like any alloy piece). They don't carry perks: those belong to relics and curios.

## Enchanted books

Every ExcellentEnchants enchantment is a reward only: no table, librarian or chest offers them. Objectives, feats and collections pay them as books, and earning one also lets you buy it again in the shop.
