The waters hold more than cod and salmon. Each time you catch a fish, there's a fair chance it's one of Wayfarer's own species instead: whichever could be biting there, then. Junk and treasure catches never change.

## Where and when

Each species lives somewhere particular: fresh water, the sea, or the brackish water where rivers meet it; in a climate from cold to hot; some only by night, some only in the rain, a few only in one place. A river takes the climate of the land around it, so a river through a jungle is warm.

Rarer fish bite less often. **Wayfarer's Bait** makes your next ten catches tempt the rare and legendary ones twice as often.

## Weights and records

Every fish is weighed when you land it, and its weight, your name, the date and the place go on it. The Almanac keeps your heaviest of each species. Land a **legendary** fish and the whole server hears about it.

They're still fish: eat them, cook them like cod, feed them to your cat.
