New metals don't come out of a furnace. Each **alloy** is melted in a **crucible** from a base metal and a **catalyst**: something that ties it to a place or a creature in the world. Every alloy has its own tools and armour, in every vanilla shape from sword to boots.

## The crucible

A crucible is an empty cauldron set over a heat source. What's underneath decides its **heat**, and a hotter crucible melts everything a cooler one can:

<Crucible />

- **Load it** by right-clicking with an ingredient; sneak to drop in the whole stack. A label above it shows what's inside and what it's doing.
- **The melt starts by itself** once the contents match a recipe and the heat is high enough. The order you add things doesn't matter.
- **Batch it.** Two or three times a recipe gives two or three times the ingots{rules && <>, up to <strong>{rules.maxIngots}</strong> in one melt</>}.
- **Mistakes cost nothing.** A wrong mix just sits there. Sneak and right-click with an empty hand to take everything back, even mid-melt; breaking the cauldron drops it all too.
- **Walk away if you like.** A melt pauses if the fire goes out and carries on when it's back, and it keeps going while you're far away, as long as the heat is still there.

Every ingot you forge is counted for whoever started the melt.

## The six alloys

**Steel** and **bronze** come first, around the time you have iron. **Shakudo** and **electrum** are for the diamond age. **Hepatizon** needs the Nether's soul fire, and **adamant** the deep dark too.

None of them beat netherite outright. Like relics, they're sidegrades, each with one thing it does best.

## From ingot to gear

At the smithing table, put in the alloy's **template**, a piece of gear and one of its ingots. Steel and bronze upgrade iron gear; the others upgrade diamond gear. Any piece of alloy gear can also move onto a different alloy the same way, so a favourite pickaxe can follow you through the stages. Enchantments, trims, names and durability all carry over.

## Alloy templates

Templates are won from **structure guardians**: everyone who helped beat one gets its alloy's template. Roaming bosses don't carry them. Once you have one, copy it at a crafting table, the way you would a netherite template: the template in the top middle, seven of its ingots around it, and the block below in the centre, for two templates.

## Repairs

Alloy gear repairs on an anvil with its **own ingots** (bronze takes plain copper too); plain iron or diamond won't touch it, and it won't combine with gear of another metal.
