New metals are coming, and they don't come out of a furnace. Each **alloy** is melted in a **crucible** from a base metal and a **catalyst**: something that ties it to a place or a creature in the world. Every alloy has its own tools and armour.

## The crucible

A crucible is a cauldron set over a heat source. What's underneath decides its **heat**:

<Crucible />

Drop the base metal and the catalyst in at the right heat and a **melt** begins. Wait, and ingots come out.

## The six alloys

**Steel** and **bronze** come first, around the time you have iron. **Shakudo** and **electrum** are for the diamond age. **Hepatizon** and **adamant** are the deep end of it.

None of them beat netherite outright; like relics, they're sidegrades, each with its own strengths. The recipes, heats and catalysts will appear here as recipe cards once they're on the server.

## Alloy templates

To turn your existing gear into an alloy's gear at the smithing table, you need that alloy's **template**. Templates are won from **structure guardians**.
