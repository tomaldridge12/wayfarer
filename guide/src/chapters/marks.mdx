The dull jobs (digging sand, shearing sheep, chopping trees) are going to start paying off. Each one gets its own ladder, counted the same way as kills, and climbing it earns a **mark**.

## What a mark does

A mark is a tool's gimmick, added at the smithing table. The **Dune Mark** turns a shovel into a 3×3 sand-digger; a shepherd's mark lets shears clip every sheep around you at once. The idea is simple: the grind pays off once, and then it's lighter forever after.

## Ranks

Marks come in **ranks**. Climb further up the same ladder and you earn the next rank, which replaces the one before (Dune Mark II is just a better Dune Mark I).

A mark stays with the tool when you upgrade it to an alloy, so nothing you earn is wasted.
