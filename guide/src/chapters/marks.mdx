The dull jobs (digging sand, shearing sheep, fishing, farming) pay off. Each one has its own ladder, counted the same way as kills, and every step up it earns a **mark**: a tool's gimmick that makes that job lighter from then on.

## Putting a mark on a tool

Climbing a ladder gives you that rank's **mark template**. At the smithing table, put in the template, a tool of the right kind (vanilla or any alloy) and the job's own material: sand for the Dune Mark, wool for the Shepherd, string for the Angler, a hay bale for the Reaper.

- **The template stays.** Take the marked tool and the template is still in the table, ready for a spare or a replacement.
- **Ranks replace each other.** A higher rank takes the place of a lower one on the same tool (Dune Mark II is just a better Dune Mark I), and a tool never takes a lower rank than it has.
- **Marks stay with the tool**, through repairs and through upgrading it to an alloy, so nothing you earn is wasted.
- **Sneak** while digging or reaping to work one block at a time.

Everything a mark does counts as if you'd done it by hand: the extra sand counts toward the Dune ladder, every extra sheep toward the Shepherd, and so on.

## Veinminer and Treefeller

Two more ladders pay enchanted books instead of marks: **Veinminer** for the ores you mine and **Treefeller** for the logs you chop. These books are the only way to get those enchantments on this server: no table, librarian or chest will ever offer them. Each level comes from climbing further.
