Old things turn up for people who look: gems, statues, charms, charts, fossils and coins. They're collectibles: put them in item frames and on shelves and build yourself a cabinet of curiosities. Each one you find goes on the Journal's Treasures page.

## Archaeology

Brush a suspicious block at trail ruins, desert wells, desert pyramids or ocean ruins and there's a **{Math.round(rules.brushChance * 100)}% chance** it holds a treasure instead of its usual find. Unlucky? Nobody brushes more than **{rules.ceiling}** in a row without one. Treasures you don't have yet turn up three times as often as ones you do, and the **Archaeologist's Brush** curio doubles your chance.

## Chests

Some treasures wait in structure chests instead, so explorers who never pick up a brush still find a few.

## Charts

A chart, used up with a right-click, shows you the way to the nearest place of its kind for ten minutes.
